How this register works

Joining is optional. Your chosen name, score, run length, conditions and scoring edition are public. This browser keeps one entry: the best of its current run scores and its best banked result. A live score can fall after a heist; earlier peaks from that same run are not saved.

All scoring editions compete on one permanent board. Longer mandates can earn more. Equal scores favor the entry that reached that score first. Points use your browser’s number formatting.

The Worker audits score accounting, not whether a human performed every browser action. Clearing cookies creates a new browser identity; names are not accounts and need not be unique. Withdraw through My participation before clearing cookies if you want your public entry removed.

When a run disconnects, only its last acknowledged score remains. An active result without a recent update is marked accordingly. Reconnecting applies the latest score, including any losses.
