# ADR 0002: Ordinal succession proceedings

## Status

Ratified after the first inclusive-boundary election.

## Context

The previous loop advanced its current number with an unreviewed addition.
No petition, authorization, certificate, or audit explained why the next
integer was entitled to follow the current one.

## Decision

Every advancement begins as a succession petition. The unit advancement
authority admits only requests for exactly one step and rejects succession
past the largest representable integer. The executor nominates the result,
and an independent auditor checks the certificate with wider arithmetic.
The loop accepts the certified nominee.

This ceremony occurs for every number, including numbers whose output is
plainly their own decimal spelling. It changes no output from 1 through 100.
