# ADR 0032: The Corporate Reorganization Machine

Status: accepted, 4 November 2026.

The existing office becomes a relocatable estate. Visitors may announce a new
reorganization in either the standalone tour or Executive Hovering. The same
configuration applies to both views. It remains optional presentation machinery:
Java's numerical decisions, response bytes, audit custody, companion assignment
and service measurements retain their existing contracts.

## Six completed stages

1. Introduce deterministic layouts, symbolic addresses and corridor routing.
2. Move the existing departments into local coordinate frames without changing
   their authored stories or evidence identities.
3. Alternate two physical shapes and install original absurd partitions.
4. Choreograph relocation and resume the interrupted observation safely.
5. Expose floorplans, management minutes, per-tab continuity and visit links.
6. Review accessibility, document the arrangement and release through the full
   validation and deployment gates.

Every stage receives its own full type, unit, JVM/browser build, parity and
browser review before the next stage begins. Intermediate commits stay on this
iteration branch; the public deployment receives the completed iteration.

## Estate and address authorities

`EstateLayoutStrategy` supplies either a two-row, four-column estate or a
three-row, three-column estate. Edition zero preserves the founding geometry.
Every new edition alternates shapes, shuffles the eight department identities,
and physically relocates all eight. A square estate allocates its ninth cell
to a reproducible atrium or breakroom at a reproducible address.

An `estate-v1` charter consists of a 128-bit seed and an edition. A strict parser
regenerates the layout rather than accepting coordinates from a URL or storage.
Candidates must have unique departments and slots, valid placements and bounds,
known partition policies and connected routes. The relocation coordinator seals
caller-supplied layouts so a replacement authority cannot change approved
coordinates after validation. The maximum supported edition is one million;
advancement beyond that produces a readable refusal.

The address book resolves a department and named station through its room-local
frame. Furniture, props, hit targets, collisions, production workbenches and
camera shots share those transforms. No world coordinates are stored in Java
evidence. The route planner uses connected corridor intersections, door aprons
and clear interior approaches. Directed shots follow those routes and approach
the current symbolic station. Arrival duration follows illustrated distance;
work and contemplation retain their separately earned time.

## Strategic boundaries

Twelve swappable partition authorities supply original procedural geometry:
giant pink hearts, jail bars, white picket fences, cheese with real holes,
Venetian blinds, tangled red tape, hedges, cardboard, filing cabinets, bubble
wrap, dominoes and waffle grids. Their management claims are supplied beside
their physical forms. In particular, the bars implement Employee Autonomy and
the hearts implement Psychological Safety Infrastructure. These are fictional
Facilities claims, not assurances or external certifications.

Geometry batches remain local to departments. Shared palettes and reusable
crates bound GPU resources across repeated moves. The ninth-cell amenities are
inspectable through both native picking and the semantic floorplan. Each has
three additional authored responses; the original thirty-two curiosities and
sixty-four responses keep their identities.

## Relocation transaction and observation

A validated candidate earns approximately 12.5 seconds of visible ceremony:
packing, labeled crates in transit, provisional unpacking, and camera return.
Hidden tabs, off-screen views and obscuring dialogs do not earn presentation
time. Reduced motion, disabled tour animation, or **Waive the Moving Ceremony**
apply the target without requiring the animated journey.

During relocation the supervisor retains its current source event, work time,
publication flag, reader pause and stepping credit. Neither its observation
clock nor the final acknowledgment can advance. When addresses change, arrival
time is rebased by fraction; completed work and hold time retain their offsets.
The same event resumes at its new station, and publication remains at most once.
Stop, a replacement mandate, Autonomy and closing the tour settle a pending
move without waiting for the optional renderer. Graphics failure leaves the
HTML directory, floorplan, minutes, inspections and numerical controls usable.

The separately bundled renderer receives immutable layout/transaction snapshots;
it does not import a second mutable estate singleton. Temporary shadow handling
and matrix unfreezing permit moving roots; normal rendering budgets return
after settling. Foundation, lip and corridor matrices are refreshed when the
shape changes; camera-facing signs remain outside the static matrix cache.
Walking returns to an approved interior position.

## Public records and continuity

The floorplan is a native list with current-location information, followed by a
full address and partition table. Management minutes retain ten consecutive
moves, each with a title, rationale, qualified old/new addresses and measured
mean illustrated desk-to-desk travel over the 56 directed department pairs.
Distances use scene units and never claim to measure Java latency. Longer
journeys improve cross-functional exposure; shorter ones trigger an efficiency
investigation. The bounded minutes region preserves readers above its bottom.

Session storage retains versioned charter tokens and their consecutive history,
not numerical records or new audit transactions. Reload resumes the tab's
approved arrangement. Invalid, future, inconsistent or unavailable storage has
a readable fallback. A valid explicit estate link takes priority. Legacy
`office-v1` links without an estate retain the founding building.

Visit URLs retain the exact decimal mandate, companion authority, room and
chosen authored reply, adding the versioned estate token. They prefill and open
an inspection; they never start counting. A committed move updates the current
address and share links. Clipboard refusal selects a readable link for manual
copying. No reorganization API endpoint exists: the optional Java-server mode
uses the same browser presentation authorities around actual server evidence.

## Validation and release

Pure tests cover thousands of generated estates, journeys, transforms, partition
solids, mutation isolation, transaction clocks, interruption generations, shot
poses and strict session/share contracts. Browser checks exercise real geometry,
repeated moves and resource plateaus, actual picking, walking, both views,
publication custody, modal holds, Stop/new mandate, reduced motion, NoGL,
keyboard access, reflow and reader ownership. The release retains all JVM/browser
parity comparisons and all three browsers with both compiled engines. The
publisher explicitly runs the complete estate test family before publication.
