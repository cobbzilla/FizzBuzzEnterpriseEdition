Retrieved: 2026-11-02 UTC. The downloaded header identifies Unicode Emoji **18.0**,
dated 2026-04-30. Builds use this checked-in snapshot and never fetch the moving

The adjacent LICENSE.txt is Unicode License V3. Copyright and source notices are
retained in the data. Glyph rendering belongs to the visitor's platform; the site
always supplies textual names for unsupported glyphs.

The fully-qualified inventory is filtered by scripts/emoji-provenance.ts. People
and body components, human faces and human-bearing signage are excluded. Flags
with human figures or body-part emblems are also excluded conservatively. The
admitted 1,364 sequences are recorded in catalog/symbols.ts; some established
editorial names are retained from the original Emoji 16.0 drafting inventory.
The other 1,836 characters combine two admitted sequences and are labelled as
themed pairs. They do not claim to add new Unicode emoji.
